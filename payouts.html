How the money works

You keep your full price

List a loaf at $7 and $7 lands in your account. Market Day does not take a cut of your price.

The buyer pays the service fee

10% plus 30 cents, shown to them as one line at checkout. That covers card processing and keeps the lights on here.

Stripe pays you directly

Money goes to your bank on Stripe's normal schedule, usually a couple of days. Stripe sends your 1099 at tax time.
