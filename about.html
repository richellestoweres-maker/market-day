Food brings us together.

Market Day connects you with the growers, bakers and producers already working a few miles from your kitchen. Reserve what you want ahead of time, then collect it from the person who made it.

Every seller is verified

Anyone can start a page here. Nobody can sell until they show us they are allowed to. A baker sends their food handler certificate, a rancher sends the inspection number from their processor, a grower confirms their produce is raw and uncut. We check it, and we open only the categories that document actually covers.

If a certificate lapses, those categories close on their own and the listings come down until it is renewed. That happens without anyone remembering to do it.

You pay the farm, not us

Reserving holds your order. Money changes hands when you collect, directly between you and the person who grew or baked it. We never take a cut of your groceries and we never handle your card.

Pickup, not delivery

Collecting in person is not a limitation, it is the point. It keeps small producers legal, it keeps the food out of a stranger's trunk, and it means you actually meet the people feeding you.
